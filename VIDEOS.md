# EDGE Pro — Short-Form Video Scripts (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)

Ready to produce — via Higgsfield, a screen recording, or your phone. Each is 8–20s.
**Rules:** hook in the first 2 seconds, one idea per video, show the product, end with the demo link.
Demo to screen-record: `https://edgeprojournal.com/app.html?demo=1`

---

## 1. "The reason you fail funded challenges" (hook: pain)
- **0–2s (HOOK, big text on screen):** "You're not failing challenges because of your strategy."
- **2–5s:** "You're failing because you break your own rules." *(quick cuts: moving a stop, revenge entry)*
- **5–12s:** Screen-record the EDGE Pro dashboard — zoom on the **Discipline Score (88)** and **Head Space** wheel. VO/text: "This grades every trade against MY rules and shows exactly where I slip."
- **12–15s (CTA):** "Free demo, no signup — link in bio." Show the logo.
- **Caption:** Most traders have a discipline problem, not a strategy problem. I built a journal that measures it. Try it free (no signup) 👇

## 2. "POV: your journal grades you" (hook: novelty)
- **0–2s:** Text: "POV: your trading journal grades your discipline, not your P&L."
- **2–10s:** Screen-record logging a trade → ticking the pre-trade checklist → the Discipline Score updating.
- **10–14s:** Show the colour-coded calendar filling in green/gold. Text: "Green = clean. Gold = you slipped."
- **CTA:** "Try the demo — link in bio."
- **Caption:** Every journal shows what happened. This one shows *why* — and keeps you honest.

## 3. "Running 3 funded accounts" (hook: your niche)
- **0–2s:** "Running multiple prop-firm challenges? Stop using spreadsheets."
- **2–12s:** Screen-record the **Evaluation** page — switch between challenges (FTMO, Apex, TopStep), each with its own target/drawdown/progress ring.
- **12–15s:** Show "Export PDF" → a clean track record. Text: "One tap = a report for your prop firm."
- **CTA:** demo link.
- **Caption:** Built for funded traders who run more than one account. Track them all in one place.

## 4. "Which emotion costs you money?" (hook: curiosity)
- **0–2s:** "I found out which emotion was costing me the most money."
- **2–10s:** Screen-record the **Head Space** wheel + Reports "By emotion" table — point at FOMO/Revenge in red.
- **10–14s:** Text: "FOMO cost me $160. Now I see it before I trade."
- **CTA:** demo link.
- **Caption:** Your emotions leave a paper trail. EDGE Pro shows it. Free demo 👇

## 5. "Spreadsheet vs EDGE Pro" (hook: comparison)
- **0–2s:** Split screen: ugly spreadsheet | EDGE Pro dashboard. Text: "Upgrade your journal."
- **2–10s:** Fast cuts through the app — equity curve hover, discipline ring, R:R distribution, tags.
- **10–14s:** Text: "Same 10 minutes. Way more edge."
- **CTA:** demo link.
- **Caption:** If you still journal in a spreadsheet, watch this.

## 6. "Build in public" (hook: authenticity — YOU on camera)
- **You talking to camera (most powerful for trust):** "I kept failing funded challenges, so I built my own journal that grades my discipline. It's live — here's what it does." Then screen-record 2–3 features. End: "Try it free, tell me what to add next."
- **Caption:** Solo-building EDGE Pro in public. Feedback welcome. Demo link in bio.

---

## Production tips
- **Screen recordings** of the demo are your cheapest, best content — no signup needed, looks pro.
- Add **captions/subtitles** (most people watch muted).
- Trending audio > silence. Keep cuts fast.
- **Always** end with "free demo, no signup, link in bio."
- Post the SAME video to TikTok + Reels + Shorts. Repurpose everything.
