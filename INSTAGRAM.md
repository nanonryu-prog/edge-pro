## 5. Posting plan & tips

- **Cadence:** 1 reel/day (repurpose to TikTok + Shorts) + 3–4 feed posts/week + daily stories (behind-the-scenes, polls, "log a trade with me").
- **Best times:** early morning (pre-market) and evenings, when traders are on their phones.
- **Engage before you DM:** like + comment on your target traders' posts for a few days first — warm them up.
- **DM cadence:** 10–20 personalized DMs/day max (see the DM scripts). Never identical copy-paste — Instagram limits spammy accounts.
- **Hashtag mix:** 3–5 big (#forex #daytrading), 3–5 niche (#ftmo #fundedtrader #proptrading), 2–3 branded (#edgepro #tradingdiscipline).
- **Creators:** when someone with an audience replies, send the creator deal (10% recurring) — that's your growth engine.
