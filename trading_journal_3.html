🎯FVG Quick Reference
BULLISH FVG (Buy Setup):
• Bottom of zone = Candle 1 HIGH
• Top of zone = Candle 3 LOW
• Entry: rejection candle closes inside zone
• Stop loss: below zone bottom - buffer
BEARISH FVG (Sell Setup):
• Top of zone = Candle 1 LOW
• Bottom of zone = Candle 3 HIGH
• Entry: rejection candle closes inside zone
• Stop loss: above zone top + buffer

⚠️Discipline Rules
NEVER trade when feeling:
😰 FOMO — price already moved, you missed it. Next setup.
😤 Revenge — took a loss and want to "make it back". Stop. Walk away.
😴 Tired — poor decisions. Sleep beats a bad trade every time.
😬 Anxious — if you're scared, your size is too big. Reduce it.
IDEAL trading state:
😌 Calm + 🧘 Patient + 💪 Confident in your setup
