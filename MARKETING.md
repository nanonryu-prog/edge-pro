## B. Platform cheat-sheet
- **TikTok / Reels / Shorts** = your #1 growth engine. Screen-recordings of the app. First 2 seconds must hook. Post daily if you can.
- **X (Twitter)** = build-in-public + FinTwit relationships. 1 post/day, reply to others.
- **Reddit** = high-intent, but read each sub's self-promo rules. Give value first. 2–3x/week.
- **Discord/Telegram** = prop-firm & trading communities. Be a regular member, not a spammer.
- **YouTube (later)** = long-form "how I passed my eval with EDGE Pro" once you have a story.

- Double down on whatever channel converts. Kill what doesn't.

## E. Hard rules (protect your reputation)
