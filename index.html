Good to know

Questions, answered

What exactly is EDGE Pro?

A trading journal built around discipline. You log each trade against your own rules, and EDGE grades how well you followed your plan — then tracks your prop-firm evaluation, your P&L calendar, and your progress with XP and levels. It's the accountability layer most traders skip.

How is it different from a normal trading journal?

Most journals only tell you whether a trade won or lost. EDGE Pro also grades how you traded — did you follow your checklist, size correctly, and stay off tilt? A profitable trade that broke your rules gets flagged, because that's the habit most likely to blow the account next time.

Do I have to connect my broker?

No. EDGE Pro is manual-first by design — logging each trade is part of the review habit. It works with any market you trade (forex, futures, stocks, options, crypto) and never places trades on your behalf.

Can I use it for my FTMO or prop-firm challenge?

Yes — that's exactly who it's built for. Track your profit target, daily loss limit and max trailing drawdown live, and build a verified 50-trade track record you can show to backers.

Is my data private?

Your journal is tied to your own account and is always yours to export or delete. We never sell your data and we never trade on your behalf.

What does it cost?

Plans start at $9/mo, with Pro at $15/mo and Funded at $25/mo. Every plan begins with a 7-day free trial and no card is required to start.
